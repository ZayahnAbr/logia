Suggestion 8

Warm paper, brown shadows, a little grain

Same layout, same coral. The page goes from cool white to paper, borders and shadows turn brown instead of grey, and covers use earthier colours. Coral stays the accent, and the coral gradient headings and dark button are kept exactly as they are now.

Now
Warm
  • Page #f3eadc, cards #fbf6ec, text #2b211b (about 13:1), muted text #685749 (about 6:1), button cream on dark brown. The gradient heading is large text, same as today.
  • Grain is a 7 percent multiply layer on the page background only, never over text containers that scroll.
  • Shadows lose the big soft blur. A 1px light top edge plus two tight brown shadows reads as paper, not glass.
  • Dark mode would use deep brown (#1c1512) with cream text instead of near-black.
