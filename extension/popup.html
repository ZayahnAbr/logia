Logia School Sync

Connect to Logia

Shown in Logia under Settings, School sync. Defaults to http://localhost:3000 for local development.

The address you sign in at to see your courses. Must be https (added for you if left out). The extension only gets access to this one site.

Detection looks at your school's sign-in page. If it can't tell, it works it out from the page the first time you sync. Google Classroom isn't synced here; connect it in Logia under Settings, School sync.

In Logia, open Settings, School sync, and choose New connection code.

Sync

Review and send

    Off by default. When on, opening the Work To Do page sends your deadlines automatically, at most every 20 minutes, without the review step.

    Works with: Brightspace, Canvas, Moodle. Experimental: Blackboard.

    Your school login stays in this browser. Only titles, courses, due dates and links are sent to Logia.
