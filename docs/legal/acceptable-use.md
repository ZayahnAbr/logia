# Acceptable Use Policy

**Company:** [COMPANY NAME] ([REGISTRATION NUMBER]). **Contact:** [CONTACT EMAIL]. **Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Logia is a place to study. Many people here are teenagers. This policy says what you may and may not put on Logia. It is part of the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms).

## You may only upload what you have the right to

Before you upload or share anything (notes, files, images, videos, room covers, stickers, chat), you must be the owner or have the right to share it.

## Not allowed

1. **Sexual content.** No pornography, nudity or sexually explicit material or chat.
2. **Anything that sexualises a minor.** No images, video, drawings, stories or chat that sexualise anyone under 18. This leads to an immediate ban and a report to the authorities.
3. **Violence.** No graphic violence, threats, or content that encourages hurting people or animals.
4. **Hate.** No content that attacks or demeans people because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar traits.
5. **Harassment and bullying.** No targeting, insulting, stalking, threatening or repeatedly bothering anyone. No impersonating another person.
6. **Self-harm promotion.** No content that encourages or glorifies suicide, self-harm or eating disorders. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a helpline (see the list at the end).
7. **Illegal activity.** No content that breaks South African law, helps commit a crime, sells illegal goods, or shares hacked material, fraud or scams.
8. **Other people's private information.** No sharing someone's ID number, address, phone number, photos, marks, messages or other personal details without their permission.
9. **Copyrighted material you lack the right to share.** This includes university or school lecture slides, past papers, memos, marking guidelines, textbooks and other copyrighted work. Sharing your own notes and your own summaries is fine. Public spaces and rooms are visible to others, so be extra careful there.
10. **Spam, malware and misuse of Logia.** No bulk or repeated unwanted messages, no attempts to break, overload or get around Logia's limits or safety checks.

## Study-with-me rooms and chat

- You may not use a webcam in Logia rooms.
- Room names, covers, chat and display names must follow this policy.
- User-hosted rooms are link-only until an admin approves them.

## AI features

Do not use AI features to produce content that breaks this policy. AI output can be wrong; see the Terms.

## Academic integrity

Use Logia responsibly and follow your school, college or university's rules, including rules on AI and sharing exam or assessment material.

## What happens if you break the rules

- We may remove content, limit features, or suspend or ban accounts.
- **Strike 1:** the content is removed and you get a warning.
- **Strike 2:** you cannot upload for 30 days.
- **Strike 3, or any child sexual abuse material:** your account is banned. Child abuse material is banned straight away and may be reported to the authorities.
- We may act faster for serious cases. See the Terms for details.
