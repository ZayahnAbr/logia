# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE}} · **Contact (including our Information Officer):** {{EMAIL}}

This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information Logia collects, why, who we share it with, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Logia is the responsible party for your information.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), and your sign-in method (email or Google).
- **Age and consent:** whether you are 18 or older or between 13 and 17, the version of the terms you accepted and when, and, if you are under 18, your parent or guardian's email address and when they were recorded as agreeing.
- **Your content:** notes, files, images, videos, flashcards, deadlines, timetable, course marks, chat messages, stickers, room covers, space names and tags.
- **About you (optional):** what you tell us during setup, such as whether you are a student and what you want to use Logia for, and your plan choice.
- **Study activity:** which spaces, rooms and tools you use, study room presence (display name and goal), and AI usage counts used to apply fair-use limits.
- **Plan information:** whether you have a Standard or Pro plan and its dates. When online payment opens, payments will be handled by a payment provider and Logia will not store card numbers.
- **School sync (optional):** if you connect a school system with the browser extension, we store the deadlines read from it. We do not keep your school password.
- **Technical data:** device and browser information, IP address, and logs needed to run and secure the service.
- **Reports and moderation:** reports about content, moderation decisions and strikes.

## 2. Why we use it

| Purpose | Basis (POPIA) |
|---|---|
| Run your account and give you the service | Needed to perform our agreement with you |
| Process your content with AI features you choose to use | Needed for the service and your request |
| Keep Logia safe: detect abuse, handle reports, apply strikes, keep records | Legitimate interest, and legal obligations |
| Age checks, parent or guardian consent for under 18s | Legal obligation and consent |
| Fair-use limits and billing | Needed to perform our agreement, and legitimate interest |
| Improve and fix the service | Legitimate interest |
| Contact you about your account or changes to the terms | Needed for the service, and legitimate interest |

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content to advertise to you.

## 3. Children

- You must be 13 or older to use Logia. We do not allow sign-up under 13.
- If you are 13 to 17, we ask for a parent or guardian's email and their agreement. Logia stores that email and the agreement. Parents or guardians can write to {{EMAIL}} at any time.
- We do not knowingly use children's information for marketing.
- Parents or guardians can write to {{EMAIL}} to see, correct or delete a child's information.

## 4. Who we share it with

We use other companies ("operators") to run parts of Logia. They may process your information only for us.

- **Supabase:** database, sign-in, file storage and realtime features.
- **Hosting provider:** the company that serves the Logia website.
- **AI providers:** when you use an AI feature, the text, image or audio you send is processed by one of our AI providers. These include Groq, Cerebras, NVIDIA and Cloudflare.
- **GIPHY:** if you search for GIFs or stickers, your search is sent to GIPHY.
- **Google:** if you sign in with Google.
- **Payment provider:** once online payment opens, the provider we choose will process payments.
- **Authorities:** we may share information with the police, regulators or courts where the law requires it or to protect a child or other person. This includes reports about child sexual abuse material.
- **Other users:** content you put in a shared or public space, a public room or a view link, and your display name in chat, is visible to others as described in the app.

## 5. Sending information outside South Africa

Our operators (including Supabase, our hosting provider and AI providers) may store or process information in other countries. We choose providers that protect personal information to a standard comparable to POPIA, and by using Logia you agree to this transfer.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account and content: until you delete it or your account, then removed within 30 days, except backups, which are overwritten as they expire.
- AI chat history: until you clear it or delete your account.
- Moderation records, strikes and reports: 12 months after the case closes, or longer if the law requires or if kept for a legal claim.
- Content preserved as evidence in a serious report: kept as long as the authorities or the law require.
- Technical logs: 90 days.

## 7. Keeping it safe

We use access controls, encryption in transit, and row-level security so people only see data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you and who has had access to it,
- correct or update it,
- delete it or stop using it,
- object to how we use it, and
- withdraw consent you gave (this does not affect what we did before).

Write to {{EMAIL}}. We may need to confirm who you are first. Some things (such as safety records or legal evidence) we may need to keep.

If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings such as your theme. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

## 10. Automated decisions and moderation

Some safety checks are automatic (for example word filters and AI screening of public spaces). An automated check may hide content or send it for review. You can ask a person to look again by writing to {{EMAIL}}.

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app.
