# Reporting content and takedown

**Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE}} · **Contact for reports:** {{EMAIL}}

If you see something on Logia that breaks our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) or your rights, tell us.

## How to report

- Use the report option in the app where it is available, or
- Email {{EMAIL}} with the subject "Report".

Please include:

1. A link to the content, or the name of the space, room or user, and a short description.
2. Why you are reporting it.
3. Your name and contact details (so we can follow up). You may ask us to keep your details private from the other user.

## Copyright and other rights complaints

If you own copyright (or act for the owner) and think your work is on Logia without permission, email {{EMAIL}} with:

1. Your full name, address, telephone number and email, and whether you are the owner or an agent.
2. A description of the work and where the original is found.
3. A link to the content on Logia.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner, the owner's agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information you gave is accurate, and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
6. Your signature (typed is fine).

We may remove or hide the content while we look into it, and tell the person who posted it. If the person who posted it disagrees, they may send a response to {{EMAIL}} and we will consider it before deciding what to do. Sending a notice you know to be false is not allowed.

## Response targets

These are goals, not guarantees.

| Type | What we aim to do |
|---|---|
| Child sexual abuse material, or a child at risk | Hide at once on seeing it, and act on the same day |
| Threats, self-harm concern, harassment | First response within 24 hours |
| Copyright and other rights complaints | First response within 2 working days |
| Other reports | First response within 5 working days |

## What we do

We review the report, may remove or hide the content, apply the strike system in the Terms, and tell you the outcome where we can. For serious cases we may contact the police or other authorities as the law requires.

## Mistakes

If your content was removed and you think that was a mistake, write to {{EMAIL}} with the details and we will review it.
