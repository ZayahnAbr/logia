# Reporting content and takedown

**Company:** [COMPANY NAME] ([REGISTRATION NUMBER]), [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. **Contact for reports:** [CONTACT EMAIL]. **Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

If you see something on Logia that breaks our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) or your rights, tell us.

## How to report

- Use the report option in the app where it is available, or
- Email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the subject "Report".

Please include:

1. A link to the content, or the name of the space, room or user, and a short description.
2. Why you are reporting it.
3. Your name and contact details (so we can follow up). You may ask us to keep your details private from the other user.

## Copyright and other rights complaints

If you own copyright (or act for the owner) and think your work is on Logia without permission, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with:

1. Your full name, address, telephone number and email, and your capacity (owner or agent).
2. A description of the work and where the original is found.
3. A link to the content on Logia.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner, the owner's agent or the law.
5. A statement that the information you gave is accurate, and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
6. Your signature (typed is fine).

## What we do

We review the report, may remove or hide the content, apply the strike system in the Terms, and tell you the outcome where we can. For serious cases we may contact the police or other authorities as the law requires.
