# Admin runbook: reports and safety

For Logia admins. Internal. The moderation queue is at `/discover/admin`. This is a working draft, not legal advice. **The attorney must confirm the exact legal duties and timelines before this is relied on.**

## General steps for any report

1. Note the time you received it and who reported it.
2. Open the content once, only as much as needed to judge it.
3. Decide: no action, remove, or remove and strike (see Terms: strike 1 removal, strike 2 a 30 day upload block, strike 3 a ban).
4. Record what you did, when, and why, in the moderation record.
5. Reply to the reporter within the targets on the Takedown page.

## Spam

- Remove the content. Strike 1 for a first offence, block repeat spammers.
- Check for other accounts posting the same thing.

## Harassment or bullying

- Hide the content. Keep a copy of the text or a screenshot as a record.
- Strike the sender. For threats or repeated targeting of a person, go straight to a 30 day upload block or ban.
- Tell the reporter what you did without sharing the other user's private details.
- If there is a credible threat to a person's safety, consider contacting SAPS. Ask the attorney when a report is required.

## Copyright

- Follow the Takedown page. Record the complainant's details, the content link and the date.
- Hide the content while reviewing. Tell the uploader. Strike on confirmed breach.
- Keep the notice and your decision on file.
- Attorney to confirm: ECTA section 77 notice contents, counter-notice process, and whether to join a recognised industry representative body (such as ISPA). Do not assert a conclusion to complainants.

## Impersonation

- Compare the account to the claimed person. Ask the reporter for proof only as needed.
- Remove the impersonating name or content, and ban if clearly intended to deceive or harass.

## Self-harm concern

- Do not treat it as a rule violation first. Someone may be in distress.
- Hide the content if it promotes or encourages self-harm. If it is a person expressing distress, do not shame them.
- If you can contact them safely, send the helpline details (the owner must confirm current numbers, see the Acceptable Use page).
- If there is immediate danger to a person, contact emergency services. Ask the attorney about any duty to act.
- Record what you did and when.

## Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or anything sexualising a child

**Act the moment you suspect it.**

1. **Hide or disable the content immediately** (so no one else can see it). Do not delete it yet.
2. **Preserve it.** Keep the file, the link, the uploader's account details, IP and upload time exactly as they are. Do not edit or rename.
3. **Do not view it more than needed** to confirm what it is. Do not open it again later. Do not download it to personal devices. Do not take extra screenshots.
4. **Do not share it** with anyone, including colleagues, friends or on chat, except the authorities.
5. **Ban the account** straight away (no strike steps). Do not tell the user details that could allow them to destroy evidence elsewhere.
6. **Report it** to the Film and Publication Board (FPB) and to the South African Police Service (SAPS). [OWNER AND ATTORNEY TO FILL IN: current FPB reporting channel and contact, SAPS station or unit and contact, and the case number process.]
7. **Record the times:** when the report arrived, when you first saw it, when you hid it, when you reported to FPB and SAPS, any reference numbers, and who did what.
8. Limit who knows. Tell the Information Officer and the attorney.
9. Look after yourself. Seeing this is hard. Take a break and talk to someone.

Until then, follow the steps above and report promptly.

## Records

Keep a log of each case: date and time, reporter, content link, decision, action taken, strike count, who handled it. Keep it only as long as the Privacy Policy retention period says and the attorney advises.
